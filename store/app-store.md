## Description

```
MileMate captures the workday so you have a mileage record when you need one.

This is not a trip logbook and not a tax dashboard. You do not classify every drive, manage a ledger, or maintain a finance app. Start the workday, do the work, and keep a defensible record of how far you drove.

Use MileMate when you visit stores, clients, or job sites and need work mileage without turning driving into data entry.

What it is
• Workday capture for people who drive as part of the job
• Background mileage while you work
• A record you can review when it is time to bill or file

What it is not
• A trip-by-trip mileage logbook
• A tax-category or deduction dashboard
• A clone of existing “mileage tracker” finance apps
```
